# Gajkesari Sales - Google Play Store Description

## Short Description (80 characters max)
Field force management app for sales teams - track visits, customers, expenses & attendance

## Full Description

**Gajkesari Sales** is a comprehensive field force management application designed to empower sales teams and field officers with powerful tools to manage their daily operations efficiently. Streamline your sales activities, track customer interactions, manage expenses, and monitor attendance - all from your mobile device.

### 🎯 **Key Features:**

**📋 Customer & Store Management**
- Create and manage customer profiles with detailed information
- Track store details, locations, and contact information
- View customer history and interaction timeline
- Support for multiple customer types (Dealers, Engineers, Architects, Contractors, Site Visits)

**📍 Visit Management**
- Plan and schedule customer visits
- Real-time check-in and check-out with GPS location tracking
- Capture visit photos and notes
- View visit history and timeline
- Track visit statistics and performance metrics

**💰 Expense Management**
- Record and categorize business expenses (Food, Travel, Accommodation, Other)
- Submit expense reports for approval
- Track expense status (Pending, Approved, Rejected)
- Filter expenses by month and year
- View detailed expense history

**📅 Attendance Tracking**
- Mark daily attendance (Full Day, Half Day)
- View monthly attendance statistics
- Request attendance regularization
- Track holidays and leaves
- Visual attendance breakdown with charts

**📝 Requirements & Complaints Management**
- Add and track customer requirements
- Submit and monitor complaints
- View task details and status
- Filter by date range
- Real-time status updates

**💼 Task Management**
- Create and assign tasks
- Track task progress and completion
- View task details and attachments
- Manage priorities and deadlines

**📊 Dashboard & Analytics**
- View sales metrics and KPIs
- Track monthly performance
- Monitor visit statistics
- Brand performance insights

**🔔 Notifications**
- Real-time notifications for important updates
- Task and requirement alerts
- Approval status notifications

**🗺️ Location Services**
- Real-time GPS location tracking
- Set home location
- Automatic location updates
- Location-based visit verification

**📸 Media Management**
- Capture photos during visits
- Attach images to tasks and requirements
- View image galleries
